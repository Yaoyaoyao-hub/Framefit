# Demo script

About two and a half minutes. Start in demo mode unless live keys are configured and you have already seen one successful live try-on. If the Demo data badge is on screen, say that the images are fixtures.

## Before you start

1. Backend and frontend are running.
2. A sample photo and sample inspiration are available, or use your own JPEG.
3. History is empty for the photo you are about to use.

## 0:00 Opening

"FrameFit takes one frame of an outfit and lets me try pieces from it, then look for something to buy."

Point to the two panes: inspiration on the left, the personal photo on the right.

## 0:20 Capture inspiration

Upload a screenshot, or show a local video paused on an outfit and use **Use this frame**, then confirm. Mention that the video file itself is not uploaded.

If you have time, point at **Share a tab** and say it asks the browser for a tab, and protected video may stay blank. Do not promise it works for every player.

## 0:45 Click an item

Click the hat box, or choose it from the item list if boxes overlap. Say the boxes are suggestions and can be corrected or drawn by hand.

## 1:05 Try it on

Click **Try on**. When the result appears, point at the new history thumbnail labeled Hat. The right pane is now that saved version.

## 1:25 Add another piece

Select the jacket and try it on again. The new image is a child of the hat version, labeled Outerwear. Both thumbnails remain.

## 1:40 Go back and branch

Click the Hat thumbnail. Say this is a preview, not a deletion. Click **Continue from here**, then try the shoes. History now shows two branches from the hat: outerwear, and shoes.

Click **Undo**, then **Reset look**. The original photo is the source again. The later versions are still in the strip.

## 2:05 Find a product

Select an item and click **Find similar products**. Open one product page. Point out **Check price** where no price was returned, and **Similar style** on a link that is not a shop page.

Click **Try this product**. The new history label ends with "product", so it is distinct from the inspiration try-on.

## 2:25 Close

"Every successful result is kept. Going back does not throw later versions away. Where the badge says Demo data, those pictures are fixtures, not live provider output."

Do not quote speed, return rates, or a claim that earlier garments always survive the next try-on. Use `docs/evaluation.md` if someone asks.
